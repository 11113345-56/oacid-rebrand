Background of OACID

Ogabwa Community Initiative for Development (OACID) is a capacity building Institution established in 2020 as a voluntary venture involving a team of experts in different developmental interventions. It was created to address gaps in intervention strategies by civil society and non-governmental organizations, which often struggle to sustain long-term impact in communities.

To formalize operations, OACID was officially registered in May 2023 as a company limited by Guarantee without share capital (Registration No: 80034190088640). The initiative was engineered by professionals with over 15 years of experience in community development, forming a strong foundation for continued support in community transformation.

Our Locations

OACID Head Office is located at Luteete, 8kms along Kampala - Gayaza Highway. We also operate a field office in Lira to coordinate activities in Northern Uganda, and a Satellite Coordination Centre in Mbale City for the Eastern region.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To promote rural development through innovations and community-led participation for sustainable impact.

Our Mission

To promote community participation in development through business support services, rural finance, climate change adaptation, ICT innovations, research, and partnerships for sustainable livelihoods.

Our Core Values

Mutual Respect

We respect our clients, partners, and all who support the drive to poverty reduction.

Accountability

We report transparently to clients, members, partners, and stakeholders.

Team Work

We achieve goals through collaboration with partners and clients.

Professionalism & God Fearing

We serve with excellence and faith in God to guide our mission.

Company Objectives

  • Provide business development support services to farmers, enterprises, and community associations.
  • Support entrepreneurship development for informal, micro, small, and medium enterprises.
  • Promote access to microfinance and rural finance through training and mentorship.
  • Advance agricultural value chains and climate-smart agriculture.
  • Promote climate change adaptation through agro-forestry and landscape restoration.
  • Leverage ICT innovations for efficiency and cost reduction.
  • Conduct research and development for effectiveness and impact.
  • Collaborate with government and partners to facilitate community participation.
