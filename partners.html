Our Partners

OACID collaborates with national and international partners to deliver impactful community empowerment programs across Uganda.

Karamoja Women Empowerment

In partnership with UNDP, the Government of Uganda, and Lead Farmers, OACID supported women in Karamoja through commercial oilseed crop production, boosting livelihoods and food security.

Business Growth & Job Creation

Through the UNCDF START Facility, OACID provided Business Development Services (BDS) to Agribusiness SMEs, promoting entrepreneurship and employment opportunities.

Strengthening BDS in Northern Uganda

In collaboration with Friends Consult and GIZ, OACID enhanced the supply and quality of affordable Business Development Services for underserved communities in Northern Uganda.

Public & Private Sector Engagement

OACID works with both public institutions and private organizations to co-design and implement sustainable development programs that empower communities and promote inclusive growth.
